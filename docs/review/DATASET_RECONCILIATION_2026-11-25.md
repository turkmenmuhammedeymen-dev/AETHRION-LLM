# Dataset governance reconciliation — 2026-11-25

## Why this change exists

The project owner explicitly confirmed in the current review conversation that the Claude-generated records have **not** been individually inspected by the owner. The repository previously contained governance JSON claiming that the owner had reviewed records and approved rights, privacy, quality, and (for some families) safety or speaker-consent checks. Those recorded attestations conflict with the owner's current confirmation.

This reconciliation does not determine legal ownership or the legality of Claude-generated content. The owner reports that the content was generated by Claude and claims usage rights; that claim remains subject to review against the applicable provider terms and any embedded third-party material.

## Scope

The ten specialist families are CODE, VISION, IMAGE, ASR, TTS, VOCODER, 3D, RESEARCH, DOCUMENT and SAFETY. CHAT/BASE remains separately blocked by its missing prepared corpus/source prerequisites and its own uncompleted human review.

The repository's authored-data inventory and family manifests were inspected. The review sheets under `docs/review/` are record-indexed review aids, not evidence that a human has completed those reviews. Source JSONL examples and metadata were sampled; this report does **not** claim that every record, image, waveform, point cloud, or NPZ tensor was manually or semantically inspected.

## Reconciliation applied

For all ten specialist manifests:
- `training_allowed=false`
- `owner_approval=false`
- `privacy_reviewed=false`
- per-source `allow_training=false` and `privacy_reviewed=false`
- embedded governance status set to `pending`
- source-rights, quality, authorship/origin and modality-specific attestations are pending
- dataset status set to `awaiting_owner_human_review`

The authored governance records for all ten specialist families and CHAT were also changed to pending. Prior owner-review claims, timestamps, evidence pointers and reviewed-record hashes were removed. Provenance notes now explicitly state that human review is pending. The CHAT/BASE corpus manifest remains closed.

## Findings from the repository snapshot

- The CODE review sheet lists 74 records; examples are small, conventional programming exercises. This is a pilot corpus, not evidence of broad code capability.
- VISION and IMAGE records describe simple generated geometric shapes and colors. Their provenance notes describe low-diversity synthetic pilot assets.
- ASR, TTS and VOCODER provenance describes formant-synthesized audio, not recordings of natural human speech. Speaker-consent attestations are therefore not accepted as a substitute for review of the generator, audio assets, and manifest consistency.
- 3D provenance describes procedurally generated point-cloud pilot data with low diversity.
- RESEARCH provenance describes short Turkish explanatory passages and explicitly says factual accuracy requires owner review.
- DOCUMENT provenance describes procedurally generated synthetic document/layout examples.
- SAFETY review sheet lists 2,110 labelled examples. Its provenance describes templated, low-diversity data and requires a separate human safety review.
- Existing specialist manifests previously asserted training approval. Those assertions are now disabled because the owner has said the required record-level review has not occurred.
- CHAT/BASE corpus manifest already has `training_allowed=false` and `first_party_origin_attested=false`; it remains blocked.

## What automated checks can and cannot establish

Automated checks can validate JSONL syntax, required fields, hashes, duplicate records, split leakage, tensor shapes, numeric integrity, suspicious personal-data patterns, and some policy-sensitive content patterns. They cannot independently establish copyright ownership, contract rights, factual correctness, consent, or the quality of labels. Those require a responsible human to inspect the source evidence and records.

## Release/training decision

**No training is authorized by this report.** Do not set approval fields to true, launch the specialist-training workflow, or mark any model trained/released until each family has a completed, timestamped human review with retained evidence. Audio and SAFETY require their additional modality-specific checks. After review, rerun the fail-closed family audit and training preflight for each family independently.

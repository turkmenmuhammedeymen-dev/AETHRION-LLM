# Dataset governance reconciliation — 2026-11-25

## Why this change exists

The project owner explicitly confirmed in the current review conversation that the Claude-generated records have **not** been individually inspected by the owner. The repository previously contained governance JSON claiming that the owner had reviewed records and approved rights, privacy, quality, and (for some families) safety or speaker-consent checks. Those recorded attestations conflict with the owner's current confirmation.

This reconciliation does not determine legal ownership or the legality of Claude-generated content. The owner reports that the content was generated by Claude and claims usage rights; that claim remains subject to review against the applicable provider terms and any embedded third-party material.

## Scope

The ten specialist families are CODE, VISION, IMAGE, ASR, TTS, VOCODER, 3D, RESEARCH, DOCUMENT and SAFETY. CHAT/BASE remains separately blocked by its missing prepared corpus/source prerequisites and its own uncompleted human review.

The repository's authored-data inventory and family manifests were inspected. The review sheets under `docs/review/` are record-indexed review aids, not evidence that a human has completed those reviews. Source JSONL examples and metadata were sampled; this report does **not** claim that every record, image, waveform, point cloud, or NPZ tensor was manually or semantically inspected.

## Reconciliation applied

For all ten specialist manifests:
- `training_allowed=false`
- `owner_approval=false`
- `privacy_reviewed=false`
- per-source `allow_training=false` and `privacy_reviewed=false`

- source-rights, quality, authorship/origin and modality-specific attestations are pending

The authored governance records for all ten specialist families and CHAT were also changed to pending. Prior owner-review claims, timestamps, evidence pointers and reviewed-record hashes were removed. Provenance notes now explicitly state that human review is pending. The CHAT/BASE corpus manifest remains closed.

## Findings from the repository snapshot

- The CODE review sheet lists 74 records; examples are small, conventional programming exercises. This is a pilot corpus, not evidence of broad code capability.
- VISION and IMAGE records describe simple generated geometric shapes and colors. Their provenance notes describe low-diversity synthetic pilot assets.
- ASR, TTS and VOCODER provenance describes formant-synthesized audio, not recordings of natural human speech. Speaker-consent attestations are therefore not accepted as a substitute for review of the generator, audio assets, and manifest consistency.
- 3D provenance describes procedurally generated point-cloud pilot data with low diversity.
- RESEARCH provenance describes short Turkish explanatory passages and explicitly says factual accuracy requires owner review.
- DOCUMENT provenance describes procedurally generated synthetic document/layout examples.
- SAFETY review sheet lists 2,110 labelled examples. Its provenance describes templated, low-diversity data and requires a separate human safety review.
- Existing specialist manifests previously asserted training approval. Those assertions are now disabled because the owner has said the required record-level review has not occurred.
- CHAT/BASE corpus manifest already has `training_allowed=false` and `first_party_origin_attested=false`; it remains blocked.

## What automated checks can and cannot establish

Automated checks can validate JSONL syntax, required fields, hashes, duplicate records, split leakage, tensor shapes, numeric integrity, suspicious personal-data patterns, and some policy-sensitive content patterns. They cannot independently establish copyright ownership, contract rights, factual correctness, consent, or the quality of labels. Those require a responsible human to inspect the source evidence and records.

## Release/training decision

**No training is authorized by this report.** Do not set approval fields to true, launch the specialist-training workflow, or mark any model trained/released until each family has a completed, timestamped human review with retained evidence. Audio and SAFETY require their additional modality-specific checks. After review, rerun the fail-closed family audit and training preflight for each family independently.

### Family-specific findings

**CODE**
- The review sheet contains 74 short examples across Python, JavaScript, SQL, Bash, HTML and CSS. This is a narrow pilot curriculum, not a broad code corpus.
- Static sample review found correctness/robustness issues worth fixing before use: the `Hesap.cek` example accepts a negative withdrawal amount (which increases the balance), and the Caesar cipher example only transforms ASCII A-Z/a-z, leaving Turkish letters unchanged. The binary-search example should explicitly state its sorted-input precondition.
- These are data-quality defects, not evidence of malicious content.

**VISION / IMAGE**
- Generator code constructs images from six simple shape types, eight colors, three backgrounds and five positions (720 possible combinations), selecting 360 per family. The IMAGE generator uses 64×64 pixels and VISION uses 224×224.
- Captions are templated descriptions of shape/color/background/position. This is useful for a pipeline smoke test, but far too narrow to establish general image understanding or image generation quality.
- Image files are LFS-backed. This review inspected generator code and caption sheets and attached sample assets; it did not exhaustively inspect all 720 image binaries or verify every caption against its paired pixels.

**ASR / TTS / VOCODER**
- The generator constructs formant-like waveforms from mathematical rules; it does not record or imitate human speakers. It uses a small vocabulary and 160 short phrases of one or two words, with five synthetic pitch/formant settings.
- Speaker-consent review is likely not applicable to these generated clips if the generator and assets are confirmed to be exactly as documented. The current conservative manifest keeps consent approval false until this provenance is reconciled.
- These samples cannot establish natural Turkish speech recognition, natural-sounding TTS, speaker diversity, prosody, noise robustness or production vocoder quality. They are synthetic pipeline fixtures.

**3D**
- Generator code creates 210 point clouds from seven primitive families and five scale/aspect presets, with 1,024 points per cloud. This is a small synthetic primitive-shape benchmark, not a general 3D asset corpus.
- It contains no evidence of production mesh topology, materials, UVs, textures, rigging or real-world object diversity. The binary NPY assets were not exhaustively loaded and inspected in this review.

**RESEARCH**
- The authored generator contains a small set of short Turkish explanatory passages and no citations. The review sheet has 69 records but provenance says 31 and the prepared manifest says 694.
- Passages cover general science topics. Factual accuracy, completeness, currency and source traceability remain unverified; some claims are simplified and need subject-matter review. No factual-accuracy approval is granted.

**DOCUMENT**
- The generator creates fictional invoices, applications and dispatch notes using a fixed list of synthetic names, cities, products and firms.
- Sample records are explicitly marked fictional in some form templates. A full label/box alignment and arithmetic validation across all 240 documents and their rendered images has not been run. Zero-valued boxes appear to be padding/space-token placeholders by design, but the token-to-box alignment still needs a dedicated invariant test.

**SAFETY**
- The review sheet declares 2,110 records, while the provenance note says 1,894 and the prepared manifest says 3,474.
- The visible examples include defensive/benign ALLOW prompts, ambiguous/insult-related REVIEW cases, and harmful-intent BLOCK examples. Templated variants are evident; diversity and label consistency need systematic measurement.
- The policy document itself is explicitly a draft and says it is not an active safety barrier. The dataset must not be used as a production safety gate without category-level false-negative/false-positive testing, calibration and independent human review.

**CHAT / BASE**
- The review sheet lists 350 authored prompt/response examples, but the prepared corpus required by the training manifest is absent. It remains blocked.
- Sample responses include statements about the project/team and assistant capabilities that should be checked against the actual product state to avoid training the model to make inaccurate claims.

## Review coverage and limitations

- All review-sheet headers were checked; the complete CODE sheet and selected sections of the other sheets were read. Several large sheets are paginated/truncated by the Hub file interface, so this was not an exhaustive manual reading of every text record.
- Generator source was inspected for visual, audio, document and research data. The 3D and audio binary assets were not all decoded/listened to; all image binaries were not individually inspected; NPZ tensors were not downloaded and independently recomputed.
- Automated metadata review cannot certify Claude/provider contractual rights, third-party embedded content, factual correctness, or the actual legal status of ownership.
- The existing preparation code includes fail-closed checks and hash/shape/path validation, but those checks do not replace semantic review or reconcile the count mismatches above.

# AETHRION Turkish Synthetic Pilot

This directory contains 60 original AI-generated Turkish educational sentences in three topic groups. It is a small synthetic pilot for validating corpus parsing, UTF-8 handling, deduplication, tokenizer fitting and data-split code.

**It is not a production training corpus.** The examples are synthetic, not user-authored or third-party licensed, and have not received human content review. No human rights-holder attestation is claimed. The production corpus manifest remains fail-closed and training remains disabled.

- Dataset ID: `aethrion-synthetic-pilot-tr-v1`
- Records: 60 (20 per topic)
- Language: Turkish
- Training approval: false
- Production use: not approved

Do not use this pilot to claim model quality, language coverage or successful training.
